
### Explanations of abbreviations

TWF (Tool Wear Failure) - Zużycie narzędzia: The cutting tool or drill bit has worn out and needs replacement.
HDF (Heat Dissipation Failure) - Awaria chłodzenia: The machine is overheating, usually because a cooling pump failed or the heat sink is blocked.
PWF (Power Failure) - Awaria zasilania/napędu: A failure in the drive belt, motor, or power supply delivering torque.
OSF (OverStrain Failure) - Przeciążenie mechaniczne: Bearings or structural elements failing because the torque/force exceeded physical limits.
RNF (Random Failures) - Awarie losowe: Unpredictable failures that don't directly correlate with the standard sensor data (often handled with a general inspection kit).
